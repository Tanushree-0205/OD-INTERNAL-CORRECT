Vel Tech On Duty (OD)
Letter Generation & Audit System

An automated administrative suite engineered for Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology. Seamlessly manage student event participation, track audit approval status, and produce formal academic request letters formatted to institutional standards.

End-to-End Workflow

How the System Works

A verified four-step pipeline from student activity intake to formal DOCX dispatch

STEP 01
📝
Intake & Logging
Enter student credentials, VTU ID, department, OD date, and verified purpose of event participation.
STEP 02
📋
Audit & Registry
Search, filter by date or department, review remarks, and toggle approval status between Pending and Processed.
STEP 03
⚙️
Signatory Setup
Specify official From signatory name, HOD designation, department, event name, and processed date ranges.
STEP 04
📄
Compliant Export
Download single or batch ZIP letters, or produce a combined letter with an official student roster table in DOCX format.
Core Architecture

Key System Capabilities

⚡
Day-to-Day OD Register
Real-time status management with quick status updates, multi-selection bulk actions, and instant filtering.
📜
Times New Roman Standard
Documents formatted strictly in 12pt Times New Roman with official university letterhead structure and routing to The Dean.
🖋️
Dynamic "From" Signatory
Interactive pre-download modal captures authorized signatories, remembers defaults in local storage, and syncs settings.
🏛️

Institutional Document Standard V2.4

Every generated document contains required justification paragraphs, event duration dates, student tables, and formal attendance regularisation clauses addressed to The Dean, School of Computing.
